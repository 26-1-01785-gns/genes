My Favorite Food ♡

Here are some of my favorite foods!

Ice Cream

Ice cream has always been my little reward. Whenever something small goes right, I treat myself with a cornetto ice cream, and when life gets hard, it’s the one thing that comforts me. That’s why it will always be my favorite.

Spaghetti

Spaghetti is what I look forward to on special occasions. There’s just something about it, especially when it’s creamy, that makes a celebration feel complete. It’s so good that I’m always happy when it’s on the table.

Pork Afritada

Out of all the viands, pork afritada is the one I love most. I like it with soft carrots and potatoes, and a sauce with just the right balance of tomato paste. It’s a simple dish, but it always feels like home to me.
