My Favorite Place ♡

Here are some of my favorite places!

Switzerland

Switzerland is one of my favorite places. I love how quiet it feels and how beautiful the nature is, from the mountains to the green views. It’s the kind of place that makes my mind feel calm.

Paris, France

Paris is another favorite of mine. I love the Eiffel Tower, and I love Miraculous, which is set in Paris. Every time I see it, it feels like a story I want to be part of.
